# Evidence Ledger

Only durable artifacts belong here. “Read chapter 4” is activity; an
implementation, derivation, design defense, measured report or corrected mock is
evidence.

## Evidence quality checks

- Is the claim precise and narrower than the artifact?
- Can another engineer reproduce or challenge it?
- Are data/workload/hardware and metric conditions present?
- Are negative results and limitations visible?
- Does a level-3/4 claim show judgment, failure handling or influence—not only a
  correct implementation?
