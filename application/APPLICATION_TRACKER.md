## Funnel diagnosis

- Few qualified roles: broaden geography/team/title or close a real requirement.
- Low outreach reply: improve relevance, warmth, proof and request size.
- Low screen conversion: reassess level/fit and top-half resume evidence.
- Technical-round failure: classify coding, domain, system, communication or
  evidence gap and retest specifically.
- Final-round failure: examine senior scope, leadership/project depth, team fit
  and role variance; do not overfit to one noisy outcome.
