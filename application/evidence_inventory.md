## Quantification recovery

Look for legitimate approximate ranges when exact figures are unavailable:

- Quality: metric and critical-slice change, human review time, defect/incident
  rate, calibration or false-alert change.
- Scale: users/devices/vehicles/streams, samples/frames, models/teams, QPS/FPS.
- Systems: p50/p95/p99 latency, throughput, memory, availability, recovery time.
- Economics: compute/storage/labeling cost, engineer time, capacity, utilization.
- Delivery: launch dates, adoption, number of teams unblocked, review-cycle time.

Never fabricate. Write “approximately,” “more than,” or a permitted percentage
when necessary, and be ready to explain the measurement.

## Gap labels

- **Evidence gap:** you did the work but have not recovered/proved the facts.
- **Communication gap:** evidence exists but the explanation is unclear.
- **Skill gap:** you cannot yet perform the task independently.
- **Experience gap:** the role needs production/organizational scope you have not
  yet held; target an adjacent level/role or seek that scope honestly.

These gaps require different actions. A course cannot fix an experience gap by
renaming a project “production.”
