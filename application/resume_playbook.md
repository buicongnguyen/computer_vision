# Senior CV Resume Playbook

## Build a master record first

Keep every truthful achievement, project, publication, talk, skill and date in a
private master document. Tailored resumes select from it; they do not rewrite
history to imitate a job description.

## Recommended structure

1. Name, location, contact, portfolio/GitHub/Scholar links.
2. Two-line profile only if it adds specific positioning.
3. Experience in reverse chronology with exact role/company/dates.
4. Selected projects/research when they add evidence missing from employment.
5. Education/publications/patents where relevant.
6. Skills grouped by demonstrated area, not an undifferentiated keyword wall.

Keep the document easily parsable: one column, standard headings, real text,
consistent dates, no skill bars, and restrained formatting. Senior engineers may
need two pages when both pages contain relevant evidence.

## Achievement formula

```text
Owned/decided WHAT for WHICH user/system at SCALE;
implemented HOW/WHY; changed METRIC from BASELINE to OUTCOME under CONSTRAINT.
```

Examples are structural illustrations—replace every fact:

- Led redesign of multi-camera inference for `N` streams; used profile evidence
  to change batching and preprocessing, reducing p95 end-to-end latency from
  `[A]` to `[B]` while holding `[quality metric]` within `[tolerance]`.
- Defined scene-grouped evaluation and `[critical slices]` for a detector;
  uncovered `[failure]`, guided data/model changes, and improved `[operating
  metric]` by `[amount]` through fleet/canary release.
- Drove a shared calibration interface across perception and platform teams,
  added compatibility/regression tests, and reduced `[incident/rework metric]`.

## Show seniority

Good senior bullets reveal decision, scope, alternatives or constraints,
cross-team leverage, production behavior, and measurable effect. “Used PyTorch
to train YOLO” states a tool and activity, not senior ownership.

Balance evidence across:

- Algorithm/model/data insight.
- Production software, scale, latency/reliability/cost.
- Architecture and ambiguous decision ownership.
- Cross-team influence, mentoring and quality mechanisms.
- Product/safety outcome.

## Tailoring procedure (30-45 minutes)

1. Annotate the job description by competency and required/preferred status.
2. Create a role evidence table: requirement -> strongest proof -> resume line.
3. Put the most relevant recent evidence first within each position.
4. Use the employer’s accurate term where it matches your work, while retaining
   concrete detail (for example “multi-object tracking,” not just “AI”).
5. Remove low-value or old detail to make space; do not remove dates/titles.
6. Check every noun/number against `evidence_inventory.md`.
7. Export PDF, verify selectable text/links/layout and have a technical reviewer
   read it for ten seconds and then closely.

## Role emphasis

- Google-style: algorithms/software engineering, large-scale data/ML systems,
  architecture, measurable product impact and cross-team leadership.
- NVIDIA: C++/Python, CUDA/GPU profiling, inference/deployment, memory/latency/
  throughput trade-offs and production-quality systems.
- 42dot: 3D perception, pose/tracking, multi-sensor fusion, real-time C++/GPU,
  data flywheel, simulation/safety/reliability and end-to-end ownership.

See `ROLE_PLAYBOOKS.md` and `42DOT_KOREA.md` for dated role evidence.

## Final checks

- Title, company, location and dates are unambiguous.
- Top half proves target fit without relying on the skills section.
- Every metric has a unit/context and is defensible in a deep dive.
- “I” contribution is clear while teammate credit remains accurate.
- No confidential or personally restricted information is exposed.
- Links open, PDF text selects, spelling/capitalization are consistent.
- Filename is professional, for example `Firstname_Lastname_Senior_CV.pdf`.
