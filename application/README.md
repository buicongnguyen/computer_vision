# Application System

Preparation and applications should become one feedback loop. A specific job
description is the source of truth; company names alone are too broad because
teams and levels vary.

## Workflow for each role

1. Save the job URL, title, location, level, date, and a personal copy of the
   description for your own reference; postings disappear.
2. Separate minimum qualifications, preferred qualifications, responsibilities,
   and team/problem signals.
3. Score evidence 0-3: none, adjacent, direct, exceptional.
4. Apply only when minimum requirements are honestly addressed; a preferred
   qualification is not always a gate.
5. Select the 3-5 resume achievements and 1-2 projects that most directly prove
   the job’s needs. Do not keyword-stuff unrelated tools.
6. Ask a recruiter/referrer focused questions about level, team, loop, location,
   language, and scope.
7. Record every interaction and convert interview gaps into the study scorecard.

## Weekly application cadence (from approximately week 12)

- Add 5-10 qualified roles, not hundreds of unscored openings.
- Make 2-4 focused outreach attempts with a relevant technical connection.
- Submit 2-5 high-quality applications, scaled to current readiness.
- Run one mock tied to the most common upcoming loop gap.
- Follow up once at a reasonable interval, then keep the relationship respectful.
- Review conversion: outreach replies, screens, technical rounds, onsites, offers.

Low conversion at the resume screen points to targeting/evidence/resume; failure
late in loops points to interview signal or role fit. Do not diagnose everything
as “study more algorithms.”
