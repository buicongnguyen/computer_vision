# Official Source Ledger

Verification date: **2026-07-11**

This file records the primary evidence used for company-specific preparation.
Job postings are mutable and may disappear, reopen, or change after this date.
Treat every role entry as a dated snapshot and re-open the live official posting
before applying.

## Source policy

Included:

- Official Google and NVIDIA careers pages.
- Official company hiring guidance.
- Official engineering documentation and company research publications.

Not treated as authoritative evidence:

- Interview-experience posts, forums, preparation vendors, scraped job boards,
  salary aggregators, and unsourced “question lists.”
- Search-result snippets when the underlying official page cannot be checked.
- A technology appearing in one role as proof that every team requires it.

## Status labels

- **Live/listed at verification:** the official careers page or official careers
  index presented the role and an application action on 2026-07-11.
- **Official 2026 snapshot:** official role content was accessible/indexed during
  this research, but application state can change and a stated minimum
  acceptance date may already have passed.
- **Official guidance/docs:** non-role source maintained by the company.

## Source maintenance procedure

When updating this file:

1. Use the canonical official URL and record job/requisition ID.
2. Record verification date and whether the page is live, indexed, or closed.
3. Separate minimum qualifications, preferred qualifications, responsibilities,
   and public hiring-process facts.
4. Paraphrase; do not copy an entire posting.
5. Update role playbooks only after evidence is recorded here.
6. Label a cross-role conclusion as inference unless every relevant source says
   it explicitly.
